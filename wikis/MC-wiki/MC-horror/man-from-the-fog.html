The Man From the Fog

The man from the fog is a Minecraft horror mod that adds a tall lanky creature known as "the man" or "the man from the fog"

This Page will cover sightings and known information about the man. You can find this mod on curse forge or modrinth. Both versions are called "The Man From The Fog" and created by M_Productions (curseforge) or Ma_Do (modrinth). They are also the creator of other very popular horror mods such as the knocker and the apollyon, they are also owner of the less popular "cursed stare"

Behavior

The Man's behavior is strange but simple and follows a few rules which tell it what to do in the sight of a player.

Brain

Before The Man is spotted by the player he will make a whistling noise to signal that he has spawned, he can also do "false whistles" in which he actually hasn't spawned. Upon the player seeing The Man, he will either continue stalking, enter aggro mode after staring for ~5 sec, or he will enter aggro mode immediately.

Modes

The Man has multiple "modes" that he can enter at any given time. His stalking mode is where his head will always face the player, like he is watching you. Another of his modes is "aggro" or agressive, this is where he will chase you and do what is described in the "attack" section

Attack

The Man attacks in a strange way, upon entering aggro mode he will apply the darkness effect to the player's screen making them unablle to see. Then he will summon a lightning strike near him or ontop of him. Then he will get on all fours and crawl towards the player very quickly. Once he arrives at the player he will attack relentlessly until he is un-aggro'd or the target dies. Once un-aggro'd he will despawn with a lightning bolt hitting him similarly to when he spawns

Stats

The Man has very high statistics, this heading will cover stats such as: damage, health, and chances of spawning.

300 HP or 150 Hearts
Attack Damage

Chase Speed
